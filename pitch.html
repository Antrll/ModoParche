MODOPARCHE

Turning solo plans into real-world group experiences — and filling local venues with zero ad risk.

Live URL: modoparche.vercel.app Stack: PWA · Supabase · Vercel · Groq AI
01 · The Problem

A two-sided gap in local leisure

For People

Hard to organize, harder to go alone

  • Group chats get messy and plans get canceled last minute.
  • Going out alone to new places feels awkward or unsafe.
  • Hard to know what is happening today near you.
For Local Businesses

Empty tables on slow nights

  • Social media ads cost money even when nobody walks in.
  • No easy way to attract groups on Tuesday or Wednesday nights.
  • Hard to track which online promos bring real people.
02 · The Solution

One live radar for plans & venues

01

Discover & Filter

Browse live group plans by category, date, distance, and real spots left on an interactive map.

02

Join or Create Fast

Open a parche in 30 seconds with location, spots, and an automatic Plan B if it rains.

03

Meet & Check In

Coordinate in the live group chat, confirm arrival by QR or GPS, and unlock venue coupons.

03 · Safety & Trust

Designed for real-world safety

01 · ID Verification

Users verify their ID once to earn the Parcero Verificado badge and host plans.

02 · Private Locations

Exact meeting spots and attendee lists are hidden from anonymous visitors.

03 · QR Identity & Safe Route

Personal QR codes confirm identity at the door, plus one-tap emergency route sharing.

04 · Host Moderation

Organizers can manage their guest list, remove disruptive members, or block users.

04 · Business Model

Zero monthly fees. Pay only for real arrivals.

Basic $0

2 plans per month, 0% commission, QR check-in. Zero barrier to try.

Villavo 10% or $2,500 COP

Unlimited plans, flash promos, coupons, and live analytics per verified guest.

Multi-Branch 8% or $2,000 COP

Volume rate for local brands with multiple venues across the region.

Key promise: If nobody arrives and checks in, the business pays $0. Monthly statements are paid easily via Wompi.

05 · Built-in AI · Groq LLM

Meet Parce, our AI guide

  • For Users: Explains how to create plans, verify IDs, use coupons, and check in.
  • For Businesses: Helps staff run Door Mode, launch flash promos, and understand billing.
  • Secure by Design: Runs via serverless API with JWT auth, rate limits, and sensitive-data blocking.
06 · Architecture & Engineering

Fast, secure, and low-cost stack

Frontend & PWA

Vanilla HTML5, CSS3 & ES2020 · Service Worker offline mode · 3 themes (Light, Mixed, Dark) · Mobile pull-to-refresh.

Backend & Security

Supabase PostgreSQL with Row Level Security (RLS), Realtime chat, Google OAuth, and Vercel Serverless APIs.

Maps & Payments

Leaflet + OpenStreetMap + Photon geocoding (zero map API fees) and Wompi payment integration.

Automated Quality

67 automated tests (Vitest unit tests + real Postgres RLS suites via PGlite) and zero ESLint errors.
